Time Bell Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026 • Time Bell Team

Time Bell ("we", "our", or "the App") is developed as an offline-first productivity and reminder application designed to help you organize daily tasks, manage recurring schedules, and maintain focus.

1. Information We Collect and Store

Time Bell stores your data locally on your device in a private SQLite database:

  • Reminders & Tasks: Titles, notes, schedules, categories, priority levels, and completion status.
  • Preferences: Theme settings, custom accent colors, alarm tones, and snooze durations.

We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, financial data, or location.

2. How Your Information Is Used

Information is used exclusively to deliver the core functionality of Time Bell on your device: to trigger scheduled alerts, display your timeline, and remember your interface customizations.

3. Data Storage & Security

All reminder records remain solely on your device. Time Bell does not use cloud sync by default. If you configure a private self-hosted sync server in the app's settings, data is synchronized only with that designated host.

4. Device Permissions

Permission Purpose
POST_NOTIFICATIONS To display reminder alerts and timer completion banners (Android 13+).
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM To guarantee reminder alarms ring at the precise scheduled minute.
VIBRATE To vibrate the device during reminder alarms when enabled.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED To reschedule pending reminders if the device is rebooted.
INTERNET To open external links (e.g. Play Store rating) or optional private server sync.

5. Third-Party Services & Tracking

Time Bell contains no third-party analytics SDKs, advertising frameworks, or tracking services. We do not sell or monetize user data.

6. Children's Privacy

Because no personal data is collected or transmitted to external servers, Time Bell is safe for users of all ages.

7. Contact

For questions or privacy inquiries, contact the Time Bell maintainers through the application's project repository or support channel.
