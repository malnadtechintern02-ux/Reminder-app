## Full Description (Max 4000 characters)

Stay organized and keep your day on track with **Time Bell**, a fast, private, and intuitive daily planner and reminder application designed to help you accomplish what matters most.

Whether managing urgent deadlines, tracking daily habits, setting recurring alerts, or timing deep work sessions, Time Bell gives you the tools to stay focused and organized without clutter or distraction.

### 🌟 Key Features

#### 📅 Schedule & Daily Timeline
- **Today at a Glance**: View all scheduled tasks, completed items, and pending reminders organized in a clear, chronological timeline.
- **Progress Tracking**: Instantly check your completion rate and daily productivity stats with visual summary cards.
- **Quick Actions**: Mark reminders complete or pending, snooze, or adjust tasks with a single tap.

#### ⏰ Reliable Alarms & Customizable Alerts
- **Accurate Timers**: Powered by exact scheduling so your reminders trigger precisely when you need them.
- **12 Built-in Melodies**: Choose from curated alarm sounds such as Morning Breeze, Soft Sunrise, Gentle Wake, Digital Pulse, and Calm Bell.
- **Vibration & Warning Alerts**: Enable pre-alarm warnings and vibration patterns so you never miss critical activities.
- **Flexible Snooze**: Customize snooze durations (5, 10, 15, or 30 minutes) to fit your routine.

#### 🔁 Flexible Recurrence & Categories
- **Repeating Reminders**: Set repeat rules for Daily, Weekly, or Monthly tasks.
- **Color-Coded Categories**: Organize tasks into Work, Personal, Study, Shopping, Health, and custom categories with customizable colors and icons.
- **Priority Levels**: Flag tasks with Low, Medium, High, or Urgent priority indicators.

#### 🍅 Integrated Pomodoro Focus Timer
- **Deep Work Sessions**: Built-in Pomodoro timer to help you conquer procrastination and avoid burnout.
- **Customizable Intervals**: Adjust focus time (15–60 min), short breaks (3–10 min), and long breaks (10–30 min).
- **Session Tracking**: Track completed focus cycles directly within the app.

#### 📆 Interactive Calendar View
- **Monthly & Weekly Browsing**: Seamlessly switch between months and inspect tasks scheduled on any date.
- **Visual Indicators**: Event markers clearly highlight days with scheduled events.

#### 🎨 Modern Design & Personalization
- **Dark & Light Modes**: Beautiful themes crafted for readability in any lighting condition.
- **Custom Accent Colors**: Personalize your interface with dynamic accent palettes or your own custom color picker.
- **Responsive & Fluid**: Smooth micro-animations and zero clutter.

#### 🔒 Privacy-First & Offline Storage
- **100% Offline-First**: All your reminders, categories, and settings are securely stored locally on your device in an SQLite database.
- **No Mandatory Accounts**: No sign-up, no login credentials, and no external tracking.
- **Data Export**: Easily backup your reminders and tasks to JSON at any time.
- **Optional Self-Hosted Sync**: Advanced users can optionally connect to their own private self-hosted backend API.

---

### Permissions Required
- **Notifications & Alarms (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`, `SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM`)**: Essential to trigger scheduled reminder alerts and alarm notifications on time.
- **Vibration (`VIBRATE`)**: Used for tactile alarm alert feedback.
- **Boot Complete (`RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED`)**: Reschedules your active reminders if your device restarts.

Take control of your schedule today with Time Bell—simple, reliable, and distraction-free.
